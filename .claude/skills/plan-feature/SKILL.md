Read the project, installed profile, toolchain, architecture, and relevant code. Copy
`.work/active/PLAN_TEMPLATE.md` to a concise slug under `.work/active/`, fill every
applicable section for `$ARGUMENTS`, classify its risk, and have the specification
critic review it. Tier 0–1 plans may become `APPROVED-AUTONOMOUS` after a passing
critique. Ask the user only for unresolved tier-2 product intent or tier-3 authority.
